School History

The St. Uriel Academy of Taguig City was founded on June 2005 by Dr. Ruben H. Opeña and Dr. Imelda B. Opeña. Its name was derived from their guardian angel, St. Uriel, who belongs to be one of the seven archangels of God. His name means “God is my light” or “Fire of God”. Also, he is known as the angel of wisdom. St. Uriel shines the light of God’s truth in a dark world.

He reminds people that hearts should burn for the love of God. It aims to inculcate lifelong learning, wisdom and devotion to God among its students. In the same year, St. Uriel Academy of Taguig City opened its doors to Preschool which was composed of ten (10) students with two (2) teachers. In 2006, it started to open Elementary and subsequently followed by High School education. It is a recognized institution by the Department of Education (DepEd). Moreover, the school was granted to have a lifetime recognition by the DepEd. At present, it offers all levels of education from Preschool, Elementary, Junior High and Senior High.
