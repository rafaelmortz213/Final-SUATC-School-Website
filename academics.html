Senior High School Tracks

Senior High School prepares students for higher education and future careers by allowing them to choose specialized academic tracks aligned with their interests and strengths.

ICT

The Information and Communications Technology strand prepares students for careers in programming, networking, multimedia, and information technology. It develops technical and problem-solving skills essential for the digital age.

Aligned College Programs:

  • Information Technology
  • Computer Science
  • Software Engineering
  • Information Systems

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences focuses on communication, society, culture, politics, and human behavior. It fosters critical thinking and understanding of human interactions within communities.

Aligned College Programs:

  • Political Science
  • Psychology
  • Education
  • Journalism
  • Public Administration

ABM

Accountancy, Business, and Management prepares students for careers in business, entrepreneurship, finance, and management. It provides foundational knowledge in economic systems and organizational leadership.

Aligned College Programs:

  • Accountancy
  • Business Administration
  • Marketing Management
  • Financial Management
  • Entrepreneurship

GAS

General Academic Strand is designed for students who are still exploring their interests. It offers a broader academic preparation with specialized subjects to help students decide on their future path.

Aligned College Programs:

  • Education
  • Communication Arts
  • Business Programs
  • Social Science Courses

Junior High School

The Junior High School program provides students with a strong academic foundation in core subjects while developing critical thinking, communication, and problem-solving skills. It prepares learners for the more specialized academic tracks offered in Senior High School.

Junior High School

Building Strong Foundations for Future Success

Our Junior High School program is designed to bridge the gap between elementary education and Senior High School specialization. Students engage in a rigorous curriculum that emphasizes academic excellence, character development, and holistic growth.

Program Highlights

  • Comprehensive curriculum aligned with the K–12 education system
  • Development of academic and analytical skills
  • Exposure to different subject areas and extracurricular activities
  • Preparation for choosing Senior High School tracks

Elementary Education

The Elementary Education program focuses on building fundamental academic skills in literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking. It aims to develop well-rounded learners while nurturing curiosity, creativity, and character.

Elementary Education

Laying the Foundation for Lifelong Learning

Our Elementary Education program is designed to provide students with a strong academic foundation while fostering a love for learning. Through a balanced curriculum and supportive environment, we prepare young learners for future academic success.

Program Features

  • Development of reading, writing, and mathematical skills
  • Introduction to science, social studies, and creative learning
  • Character formation and values education
  • Supportive learning environment that encourages participation
Learn More

Nursery and Kindergarten

The Nursery and Kindergarten program provides young learners with a nurturing environment where they can explore, discover, and develop essential early learning skills. The program focuses on social development, creativity, and the foundations of reading and numeracy.

Nursery & Kindergarten

A Gentle Start to Lifelong Learning

Our early childhood program creates a safe and stimulating environment where young minds can flourish. Through guided play and structured activities, we help children build confidence, social skills, and a love for learning from their very first day.

Program Highlights

  • Early childhood development through play-based learning
  • Development of communication and social interaction skills
  • Introduction to basic reading, writing, and counting
  • Activities that support creativity, curiosity, and confidence
