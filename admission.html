Enrollment Process

Follow these steps to complete your enrollment.

1

Visit the School

Students and parents may walk in to the Registrar's Office to begin the enrollment process. The school is open only for weekdays from 8 AM - 4 PM.

2

Submit the Requirements

Submit the necessary documents based on the student's educational level as listed in the Admission Requirements section.

3

Choose Enrollment Type

Students may proceed with one of the following options:

  • Regular Enrollment Students who wish to enroll as regular students may proceed with the required downpayment to confirm their enrollment.
  • TLC Scholarship Applicant Students who wish to apply for the Taguig Learners Certificate (TLC) Scholarship may request a scholarship application form from the registrar and complete the required information.
  • DepEd SHS Voucher Applicant Senior High School students may apply for the DepEd Senior High School Voucher Program by filling out the required form at the registrar.
4

Complete Registration

After submitting the documents and completing the necessary steps, the registrar will finalize the student's enrollment.

Voucher Program for Incoming Grade 11

Requirements

  • A Grade 10 graduate from any PUBLIC SCHOOL, S.Y. 2025–2026
  • Grade 10 Card
  • Birth Certificate (PSA Xerox Copy)

Benefits

  • FREE Tuition and Miscellaneous Fees
  • FREE Cloth for School Uniform, ID, P.E. T-Shirts, and Notebooks
  • NO Top-Up

Taguig Learners' Certificate (TLC) – Incoming Grade 7

Qualifications

  • A Grade 6 graduate from any PUBLIC SCHOOL in Taguig City only, S.Y. 2025–2026
  • Has spent at least 3 years in the school
  • One (1) parent must be a registered and active voter in Taguig City
  • Must be a resident of Taguig City for not less than 3 years

Benefits

  • FREE Tuition and Miscellaneous Fees
  • FREE Cloth for School Uniform, ID, Notebooks, Books, and P.E. Uniform
  • ₱2,000 allowance per year
